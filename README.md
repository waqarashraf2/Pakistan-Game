# 🛺 Pakistan-Game (Miracle of Pakistan)
*(معجزۂ پاکستان: روڈ کے بیچ میں بجلی کے کھمبے)*

A 3D endless highway traffic game built with **Three.js** and **WebGL**, featuring authentic Pakistani road culture, vehicles, and the signature challenge: **WAPDA electric utility poles directly inside highway driving lanes!**

---

## 🌟 Key Features

1. **⚡ The Miracle Obstacle (WAPDA Bijli Ka Khamba):**
   * Weathered octagonal concrete utility poles situated right in the traffic lanes.
   * Cross-arms, ceramic insulators, transformer box, warning stencil (*"خطرہ ۱۱۰۰۰ وولٹ - واپڈا"*), and sagging overhead power lines.
   * Proximity radar alert: *"⚠️ WAPDA KA KHAMBA AAGE HAI! ⚠️"*

2. **🚗 Authentic Pakistani Garage & Traffic:**
   * **Tuk Tuk Rickshaw:** Real 3D GLB model with PBR metallic green chassis, fabric yellow canopy, authentic mudflap (*"DEKH MAGAR PYAAR SE! ❤️"*), and dynamic banking physics.
   * **Honda CD 70:** Fast motorcycle with red fuel tank, Honda wing stripes, 4-stroke engine purr, and agile handling.
   * **Suzuki Mehran ("The Boss"):** The iconic Pakistani 800cc hatchback cutting through traffic.
   * **Pakistani Bedford Art Truck:** Intricately carved wooden crown (*"Taj"*), vibrant truck art side panels, Urdu poetry (*"فاصلہ رکھیں ورنہ پیار ہو جائے گا"*), and swinging metal bumper chains.
   * **🚶‍♂️ Pedestrians Crossing:** Traditional pedestrians in Shalwar Kameez and waistcoat walking across lanes. Honking the horn makes them hurry across!

3. **🔊 Authentic Vehicle Audio Engine:**
   * Authentic 2-stroke Tuk Tuk Rickshaw engine audio loop.
   * 4-stroke Honda CD 70 engine throttle roar.
   * Pakistani multi-tone musical Pressure Horn (*"Paaan-pi-pooon-paaan"*).
   * Metal impact crunch collision effect.

4. **🏙️ Pakistani Highway Environment:**
   * Al-Madina Highway Dhaba with charpais and tea kettle.
   * Commercial plazas with Urdu market hoardings, rooftop Sintex water tanks, and AC units.
   * Roadside billboards (*Tapal Danedar*, *Sohrab Cycles*, *Servis Tyres*, *WAPDA Advisory*).
   * Collectible hot Karak Chai cups for bonus distance (+50m).

---

## 🎮 How to Play

### Controls:
| Action | Key |
|---|---|
| **Steer Left** | `A` or `⬅️` |
| **Steer Right** | `D` or `➡️` |
| **Accelerate** | `W` or `⬆️` |
| **Brake** | `S` or `⬇️` or `SPACE` |
| **Pakistani Pressure Horn** | `H` |
| **Mute / Unmute** | Click Sound Icon (Bottom Right) |

*Full touch controls available on mobile devices.*

---

## 🚀 Running the Game

1. Double-click **`Play_Game.bat`** or open **`index.html`** in any modern web browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Brave).
2. Select your vehicle in the Garage (Rickshaw, CD 70, Mehran).
3. Click **"BISMILLAH! CHALAIN"** and enjoy the ride!
